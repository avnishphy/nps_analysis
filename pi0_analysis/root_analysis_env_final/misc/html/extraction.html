Stage 6 · physics observable

From corrected yields to U, LT and TT

Two extractors are available: a conventional data/SIMC ratio using the ported SIMC model, and a global generated-to-reconstructed response fit that removes the original hadronic model factor.

1. The fitted angular structure

At one beam/ε setting, the unpolarized virtual-photon cross section is written:

d²σ/(dt dφ) = [U + √(2ε(1+ε)) LT cosφ + ε TT cos2φ] / (2π)
U ≡ σT + εσL

One setting cannot independently separate T and L. The legacy CSV field name TL means LT. Coefficients are stored in μb/MeV²; display plots multiply by 10⁹ to show nb/GeV².

2. Required inputs and selections

Combined data

Requires (Q²,t,t_{min},x_B,φ,W,M_X), pi0_weight, scale, charge, run and optionally helicity. The data weight begins with pi0_weight × scale.

Matched simulation

Requires reconstructed and vertex kinematics, full_weight, sigcm, missing mass, event/channel identity and a matching raw SIMC entry when truth fields are absent from the reduced tree.

Data are divided by target factor 0.584 ± 0.014 by default. Its relative uncertainty is stored as a common rank-one normalization covariance, not independent noise in each bin.

3. Conventional SIMC-model ratio

excl_xsec_pi0_analysis_simc_model.C compares data and SIMC yields in each φ bin, evaluates the ported high-W π⁰ model at one full-weighted reconstructed reference point for each ((Q²,x_B,t′)) slice, and applies:

σbin = (Ydata/YSIMC) · [∫φ-binσmodel(reference,φ)dφ / Δφ]

The angular average is analytic and uniform in φ. The old model_sigcm_full_weight_mean remains diagnostic only. W<2 GeV is rejected because the MAID branch is not ported.

Model dependence. Acceptance and bin-centering depend on the simulated prior. A model-variation/iteration study is needed to quantify that dependence.

4. Global migration-response fit

excl_xsec_pi0_analysis_no_simc_model.C constructs basis-weighted response columns from generated coordinates while assigning observed rows by reconstructed coordinates. For reconstructed row r, truth bin b and component a:

Arba = Σevents r←b (full_weight/sigcm) · [1, √(2ε(1+ε))cosφ, εcos2φ]a /(2π)
Ŷr = Σb,aArbaσba

This removes the original hadronic sigcm while preserving generation volume, luminosity convention, radiation, detector acceptance and migration carried by full_weight. No extra Hand flux or reconstructed-bin-width division is added.

The solver whitens by uncertainty, scales columns for numerical units, and uses full SVD. Unsupported or rank-deficient published bins fail rather than being silently regularized. Default behavior has no smoothing, prior, singular-value truncation or positivity constraint.

5. Feed-in and off-diagonal migration are explicit

Adaptive (Q²,x_B,t′) boundaries are frozen once and shared between truth and reconstructed coordinates. Truth outside the reported range can migrate inward through six disjoint guard regions: t′ low/high, then Q² low/high, then xB low/high. Each populated guard carries free U/LT/TT nuisance coefficients.

The displayed “response components” are basis-weighted yields, not normalized probabilities. LT and TT entries can be negative because they are interference coefficients. Separate plots show column- and row-normalized selected-event migration fractions.

Coordinate discipline. Cuts and observed rows use reconstructed values. Harmonics, ε and coefficient columns use generated values. Falling back to reconstructed coordinates for the truth model would erase the migration correction the method is built to retain.

6. Statistical and finite-MC covariance

Data variance is the sum of squared event weights. Simulation variance retains event-level outer products of the three angular basis terms, including their correlations. Generalized least squares iterates the parameter-dependent MC variance to convergence. The covariance is conditional on final weights and is not inflated by χ²/ndf.

Optional --positive-xsec constrains the complete angular cross section to remain nonnegative across φ. When constraints bind, central values are returned but the usual covariance/error bars are deliberately unavailable; unconstrained curvature is only a diagnostic.

Included

  • Weighted data statistics
  • Finite-MC basis correlations
  • Exterior feed-in nuisance covariance
  • Common target-factor covariance

Not yet complete

7. Reading the plots correctly

  • Slice plot: observed and predicted φ dependence; the constant, cosφ and cos2φ curves are U, LT and TT contributions in the adopted basis.
  • σ vs −t′: reports generated-bin coefficients at response-weighted means; there is no additional bin-centering correction.
  • GK/PARTONS points: comparisons only, converted by the full Hand flux and Fourier projection; they are not fit inputs.
  • LT′: unavailable in the SIMC-model method because helicity luminosities and beam polarization are absent from its input contract.
  • Negative interference: signed LT/TT is normal; only a negative full angular cross section is physically problematic.
