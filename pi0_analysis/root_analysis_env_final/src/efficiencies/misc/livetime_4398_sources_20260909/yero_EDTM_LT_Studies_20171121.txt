                    What are scaler reads?


         The DAQ can be configured for scalers to be
  read out at intervals of time/event counts during a run


   Example: scaler counts = 1000 events, t-scaler = 2 sec. ,
every 1000 events or a 2-sec period, the SCALERS will produce
                   an inhibit signal (RED)
