        e.g. KinC-x50-2 or -3.

A.1     HMS Cherenkov
In choosing the Cherenkov gas, we want to maximize the Cherenkov efficiency, while also
excluding pions in all settings with a high π/e ratio. From Table 5, all of the settings with
pi/e ≫ 1 occur for beam HMS energies < 5 GeV. Even for settings with π/e ratios ≈ 1 the
HMS energies are 5.27, 4.88, and 5.27 GeV. CO2 gas at 1 atm pressure (or slightly below)
provides a pion threshold of 4.9 GeV/c.
    Current gas (from previous run) is C4 F8 O. At pressure 0.45 atm, effective pion threshold
is 4.02 GeV. Reported number of photo-electrons at 0.4 atm (absolute) is 11.
